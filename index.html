Process

How an Engagement Runs

  1. 1

    Intro Call

    30 minutes. You describe the problem, the systems involved and the deadline. I tell you if I am the right fit.

  2. 2

    Elicitation

    Workshops with the process owners. I map the current state, find the gaps and challenge requirements that do not hold up.

  3. 3

    Specification

    SRS or HLE, BPMN, wireframes, user stories and acceptance criteria. Every requirement gets an ID and a test.

  4. 4

    Build and UAT

    I answer the team's questions during the build, triage defects with QA and run UAT with the business.

  5. 5

    Release and Handover

    Release readiness, user guides and training, and documentation your team keeps.

FAQ

Questions Clients Ask

How do you work with clients outside Serbia?

As an independent contractor. I invoice monthly through my registered business in Serbia, so we sign a B2B services contract. No employment, sponsorship or relocation is involved.

Which hours do you work?

Central European Time, from Belgrade. That covers EU business hours and overlaps with almost the whole UK working day.

What will I have at the end of an engagement?

Documents your engineers and testers can use without me in the room: SRS or HLE, BPMN diagrams, wireframes, user stories with acceptance criteria and a traceability matrix. The Order Risk Hold work sample shows the format.

Which tools do you use?

Jira and Confluence for delivery, Draw.io, Lucidchart, Miro and Figma for diagrams and wireframes, Postman for APIs and Power BI for reporting. I use Claude Code and Claude Cowork every day, and I check every output before it goes out under my name.

Do you take full-time roles?

Yes, for the right team: remote, or on-site and hybrid in Belgrade. Freelance B2B contracts are my default.

How much do you charge?

It depends on scope and hours per week. I send a quote after the intro call.

When can you start?

Within 3 weeks of signing.

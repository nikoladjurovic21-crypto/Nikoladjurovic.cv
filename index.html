Process

How an Engagement Runs

Six stages, from the first call to the weeks after go-live. Change requests during the build loop back into the specification, and hypercare keeps the release under watch until it runs on its own.

  1. Intro Call

    30 minutes on the problem, the systems and the deadline.

  2. Discovery

    Workshops with process owners. AS-IS BPMN and a gap list.

  3. Specification

    SRS or HLE, user stories with acceptance criteria, wireframes.

  4. Build and UAT

    Answers for the team, test cases and defect triage with QA, UAT sign-off.

  5. Release

    Release readiness, deployment planned with DevOps, user guides and training.

  6. Post-Release Hypercare

    I watch production and adoption with support and DevOps, triage what comes in and close out with a hypercare report.

FAQ

Questions Clients Ask

How Do You Work with Clients Abroad?

As an independent contractor. I invoice monthly through my registered business in Serbia, so we sign a B2B services contract. No employment, sponsorship or relocation is involved.

Which Hours Do You Work?

I am based in Belgrade (CET) and work with clients worldwide. I shift my hours so your team gets a daily overlap.

What Will I Have at the End of an Engagement?

Documents your engineers and testers can use without me in the room: SRS or HLE, BPMN diagrams, wireframes, user stories with acceptance criteria and a traceability matrix. The Order Risk Hold work sample shows the format.

Which Tools Do You Use?

Jira, Confluence and Notion for delivery, Figma, Miro, Lucidchart, Draw.io and Balsamiq for diagrams and wireframes, Stitch and Claude Design for fast UI prototypes, Postman for APIs and Power BI for reporting. I use Claude Code and Claude Cowork every day, and I check every output before it goes out under my name.

Do You Take Full-Time Roles?

Yes, for the right team: remote, or on-site and hybrid in Belgrade. Freelance B2B contracts are my default.

How Much Do You Charge?

Rates depend on the engagement and scope, so I share them on the intro call or by email. Get in touch.

When Can You Start?

Within 2 weeks of signing, often sooner.

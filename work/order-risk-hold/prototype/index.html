What the System Does

    Rules in This Release

    • High: Risk Hold. Shipment blocked. Only the Risk Override right releases it (FR-04, FR-06).
    • Medium: Risk Hold. Shipment after a confirmation with a reason (FR-05).

    • Low or none: Open, as today.

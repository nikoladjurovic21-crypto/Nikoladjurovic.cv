      Frequently Asked Questions

      How long do I need to stay?

      Five to seven days for implants, then a second short visit for the final crowns.

      Is the price fixed?

      Yes. The treatment plan you accept states the full price, including transfers.

      What if something goes wrong at home?

      Work is guaranteed for five years, and the clinic covers the follow-up visit.

Booking.dentist: a Medical-Tourism Marketplace, Built 0 to 1

I was the only business analyst and product owner on a two-sided B2B/B2C marketplace, from discovery and funding to two live products.

Discovery

I ran customer discovery with the Mom Test, then specified the business rules the marketplace runs on: booking, cancellation and multi-currency pricing.

Two Products, One Data Model

The consumer booking app was mobile-first, with online payments and multi-currency checkout. The clinic back-office let clinics run their side of the marketplace. I specified both on one shared data model and rules engine, so a rule changed once applied to both sides. The backend ran as microservices, and I worked with commercetools, Contentful and Salesforce Commerce. I owned UAT for both products.

Scope and Funding

I wrote the business case, KPIs and MVP roadmap, built the investor pitch decks and led incubator and C-level meetings. The company raised EUR 250,000 in seed funding through Katapult. To protect the launch date and the runway, I cut 348 hours of non-MVP scope through prioritisation.

Sales Function

With the CEO I built the sales process, the CRM and the commission plan, and we managed the 10-person sales team together. We tracked conversion rate and deals closed, and both improved. Revenue grew 30%+ while I was there.

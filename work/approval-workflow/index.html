Approval Web Application for a Global CPQ Platform

A 0 to 1 approval application on the quote-to-order platform of a Fortune 500 manufacturer. It is live in 160+ countries.

Fortune 500 manufacturer, via Soprex

The Job

Quotes on the platform that need sign-off pass through several approval tiers. The approval application is where approvers review a quote and decide. It had to serve a sales organisation spread over 160+ countries, and every decision had to be traceable for SOX and ISO 9001.

What I Did

  • Ran the requirements workshops and documented the approval rules as TO-BE BPMN.
  • Specified the multi-tier sign-off: who approves at which tier, what they see, and what happens on approve or reject.
  • Built the requirements traceability matrix that links each requirement to its tests, for the SOX and ISO 9001 audit trail.
  • Wrote the REST and JSON integration contracts with SAP, Salesforce, Snowflake and SAP CPI.
  • Took the features through UAT and release readiness with QA.

Result

The application went from nothing to live use across 160+ countries, with full traceability from requirement to test. Release quality matched the rest of my work on the platform: launches held at 0 critical defects.

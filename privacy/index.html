Privacy

Short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. The one third party is Calendly, and only when you click to book a call. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Who Runs This Site

What Is Collected

  • Browsing: nothing by me. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels. Fonts and images load from this site, not from third parties.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses for security, under the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Email: if you email me, I keep the conversation to reply and, if we work together, for the contract and invoicing.
  • Booking a call: the "Book a Call" buttons open Calendly. Calendly loads only when you click, may set its own cookies, and the details you enter go to Calendly under the Calendly privacy notice, and to me so I can hold the meeting.
